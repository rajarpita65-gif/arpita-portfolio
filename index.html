AI Fruit Nutrition Scanner

A web app that works out which fruit is in a photo (or on your webcam) and estimates its nutrition from the weight you type in. The Flask backend does the image analysis, and Firebase stores the scan history.

View on GitHub ↗
  • Upload a photo or point your camera at a fruit and it analyzes it automatically.
  • To identify the fruit it uses an image-recognition pipeline with fruit localization and classification fallbacks.
  • Enter the weight and it estimates calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber and sugar.
  • Each result comes with the model’s confidence and a calorie classification.
  • Scans are saved to Firebase so you can see recent ones, and recent history is cached in the browser so it loads faster.
